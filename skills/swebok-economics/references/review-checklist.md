# Software Engineering Economics — Review / Audit Checklist (SWEBOK v4, KA 15)

Produce a findings report: per-topic coverage, a defect/gap list with severity, and concrete fixes. Anchor findings to the topic map.

## Decision gates
- Is the **real problem** framed (not just a favored solution)?
- ≥ 2 alternatives incl. **do-nothing**, judged on **explicit criteria**?
- Cash flows **discounted to present value**; horizon & MARR stated?
- **Assumptions surfaced** and **sensitivity-tested**?
- Intangibles & opportunity cost acknowledged?
- Monitoring plan for the chosen option?

## Severity
- Critical: single-alternative or un-discounted comparison driving a large commitment.
- Major: hidden assumptions; no sensitivity analysis.
- Minor: presentation.
