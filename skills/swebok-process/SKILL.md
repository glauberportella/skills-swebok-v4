## Core vocabulary (use it precisely)

A **software life-cycle process (SLCP)** organizes activities from concept to retirement. **Process models / life-cycle models** are paradigms (waterfall, incremental, iterative, spiral, agile, hybrid) chosen and **adapted (tailored)** to context — there is no universal best.

**Assessment & improvement** is deliberate: **GQM** (goal→question→metric), **framework-based** methods (e.g. CMMI/ISO 15504-style), and **agile** improvement (retrospectives, empiricism).

## Mode: Advise

- Pick the life-cycle **paradigm to fit uncertainty & feedback needs**: high uncertainty/fast feedback → iterative/agile; stable, regulated → more plan-driven; most real projects are **hybrid**.
- **Tailor** the process to context (team size, risk, domain); adopting a framework wholesale is a smell.
- Improve with **evidence**: define goals, measure (GQM), change one thing, re-measure.
- Distinguish **process** health from **product** health — improving process should move product outcomes, or it's ceremony.
