# Software Engineering Process — Review / Audit Checklist (SWEBOK v4, KA 10)

Produce a findings report: per-topic coverage, a defect/gap list with severity, and concrete fixes. Anchor findings to the topic map.

## Fit gates
- Life-cycle paradigm **justified against context** (uncertainty, feedback, regulation)?
- Process **tailored**, not copy-pasted from a framework?
- Entry/exit criteria and cadence defined?

## Improvement gates
- Improvement driven by **goals + metrics** (GQM), not fashion?
- Process metrics linked to **product outcomes**?
- Agile: are retrospectives producing tracked, closed actions?

## Severity
- Critical: process actively fighting the work (heavyweight on high-uncertainty project, or vice-versa).
- Major: framework adopted wholesale without tailoring; unmeasured “improvement”.
- Minor: ceremony hygiene.
