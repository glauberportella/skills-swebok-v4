# Software Engineering Management — Review / Audit Checklist (SWEBOK v4, KA 9)

Produce a findings report: per-topic coverage, a defect/gap list with severity, and concrete fixes. Anchor findings to the topic map.

## Plan gates
- Scope & success criteria explicit and negotiated?
- Estimates method-based with **ranges + assumptions**?
- **Risk register** live (owners, triggers, responses)?
- Measures **tied to decisions** (GQM), few and actionable?
- Trade-offs among scope/schedule/cost/quality made explicit?

## Execution
- Monitoring/control loop closing (plan vs. actual → corrective action)?
- Supplier/contract risks managed?
- Closure criteria defined (not just “ran out of time”)?

## Severity
- Critical: no risk management; single-point estimates driving commitments.
- Major: vanity metrics; no monitor/control loop.
- Minor: reporting cadence.
