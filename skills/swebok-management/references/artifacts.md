## Lightweight project plan
```
1. Scope & objectives; success criteria
2. Deliverables & milestones
3. Estimates (method, ranges, assumptions) — effort/schedule/cost
4. Resources & responsibilities
5. Risk register (top risks + responses)
6. Quality approach
7. Measurement plan (few metrics, tied to decisions)
8. Reporting cadence
```

## Risk register row
```
Risk | Probability | Impact | Exposure | Trigger | Response (avoid/mitigate/transfer/accept) | Owner
```

## GQM sketch
Goal → Questions that show progress → Metrics that answer them (and the decision each informs).
